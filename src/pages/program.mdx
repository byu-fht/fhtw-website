# Program

The 2027 workshop will be held on **1 March 2027**. The program will be posted
here after acceptance notifications go out on 9 February 2027, so please check
back in mid-February.

Interested in presenting? Submissions are due **30 January 2027**; see the
[Call for Papers](/call-for-papers).

To get a sense of the talks, browse the [2026 program](/archive/2026), which
includes abstracts, papers, and slides, or any past year in the Archive menu.
